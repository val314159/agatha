
3Demo Script: Agatha

Working premise:
Agatha is a local, voice-driven executive assistant. She has a face, a voice,
memory, a live todo system, and the ability to operate on the local machine
when explicitly asked.


1. Introduction (1 minute)

Presenter:
  Hi, my name is Joel Ward and my entry into the AI Agents Hackathon is
  my "AI Generative Assistant That's Hella Awesome": AGATHA for short.

  Agatha is a local, voice-driven executive assistant. She is not just a chatbot.
  She has a face, a voice, memory, a live todo system, and the ability to run
  commands on my local machine when I ask her to.

  The goal is simple: I should be able to talk to my computer the way I would
  talk to a human assistant. She can help me capture work, organize it, remember
  context, and run commands on my local machine.

  [Hit Hold to Talk button]

Presenter to Agatha:
  Agatha, how's it going?

Agatha:

  I’m Agatha, your ultimate personal task management assistant! I'm designed to help you stay organized and on top of your tasks with ease.
  With Agatha, you can create, update, and complete tasks effortlessly.
  You can speak to me naturally. I can remember context, manage your to-do list, organize work into workflows, track what’s blocked, and even run local commands on your computer.
  I keep track of your task dependencies and provides a clear overview of what needs your attention next.
  Whether you're managing a complex project or just trying to keep on top of your daily tasks, I'm here to make sure nothing falls through the cracks.

  Think of me as an executive assistant built into the machine itself.

  I’m a friendly interface for your working life.

4. Explanation (30 seconds)

Presenter:
What's happening behind the scenes is that Agatha is connected to a local
application stack. The browser captures my voice, the audio gets transcribed,
the supervisor routes the turn, stored context can be retrieved, the language
model generates the response, and the live todo interface updates as state
changes.

For the audience, the important point is not the names of the services. The
important point is that this is not just a text box. It is voice, memory, local
actions, and live work state tied together.


5. Conclusion (30 seconds)

Presenter:
So Agatha is an executive assistant for a local machine: conversational,
embodied, stateful, and able to help operate the environment I am working in.

She can remember context, manage todos, explain dependencies, and run commands
when I ask her to.

That is the direction I think personal assistants should move: not just better
answers, but better help with the actual work happening on the machine.
