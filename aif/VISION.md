# Agatha Vision

> **Building the future of human-AI collaboration.**

Agatha began with a simple question:

**What if an AI assistant behaved less like a chatbot and more like a collaborative partner?**

Today's assistants are built around requests and responses. You ask a question. They answer. The conversation ends. The next prompt begins.

Humans don't work that way.

Real collaboration is continuous. We interrupt each other. We switch tasks. We open documents. We sketch ideas. We notice opportunities. We remember conversations. We collaborate across hours, days, and months—not just individual prompts.

Agatha exists to explore what comes after the chatbot.

---

# Principles

## Realtime First

Conversation should be continuous.

Speaking, listening, thinking, reasoning, and acting shouldn't be isolated phases. They should happen together.

The best assistant isn't the fastest one to answer—it's the one that feels present.

---

## Voice Is the Default

Typing is important.

Voice is natural.

Agatha is designed to be someone you can talk with while you're building software, writing documents, researching ideas, or simply thinking out loud.

---

## Beyond Tool Calls

Most assistants "call tools."

Agatha works with **Surfaces**.

A Surface is a live application the assistant can understand and interact with alongside you. A to-do list, a research notebook, a browser, a design tool, or something that doesn't exist yet.

Instead of returning JSON describing what should happen, Agatha participates directly in the work.

---

## Memory Should Grow

Memory isn't just a conversation history.

It is a living representation of shared understanding.

Good memories fade when they should, strengthen when they matter, connect across topics, and evolve over time.

Agatha is designed around long-term collaboration, not isolated sessions.

---

## Everything Is Connected

Conversation.

Voice.

Tools.

Applications.

Memory.

Reasoning.

They are not separate systems stitched together after the fact.

They are different views of the same collaborative process.

---

## Humans Stay in Control

Agatha is designed to amplify human capability—not replace it.

The goal is not autonomy for its own sake.

The goal is better thinking, better decisions, and better execution through collaboration.

---

# Open by Design

Agatha is open source because ideas improve when they are shared.

We want developers, researchers, students, designers, and curious builders to experiment, challenge assumptions, and contribute new ideas.

The project will evolve in public.

Expect rough edges.

Expect APIs to change.

Expect experiments to fail.

That's part of the process.

---

# Building in Public

Development follows a simple rhythm:

* **Monday:** New release
* **Wednesday:** Architecture article
* **Friday:** Demo video

Every week brings visible progress.

Some weeks that means new capabilities.

Some weeks it means removing complexity.

Some weeks it means admitting an idea didn't work.

The journey matters as much as the destination.

---

# What Success Looks Like

Success isn't measured by benchmarks alone.

Success is when someone says:

> "I wish every AI assistant worked like this."

Success is when developers build new ideas on top of Agatha.

Success is when researchers challenge and improve the architecture.

Success is when people stop thinking of AI as a chatbot and start thinking of it as a collaborator.
